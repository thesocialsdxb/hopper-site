# Hopper

The support page and privacy policy for Hopper, the capture and task app for
iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

- Support: https://thesocialsdxb.github.io/hopper-site/
- Privacy: https://thesocialsdxb.github.io/hopper-site/privacy/

The pages are kept in the app's own repository, under `site/`, and copied here
when they change, so what the app links to and what the App Store lists are
never out of step with the build.
